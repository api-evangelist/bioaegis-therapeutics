BioAegis Therapeutics, Inc. is a privately held, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered at the NJ Bioscience Center in North Brunswick, New Jersey. Founded in 2011 around the discovery work of the late Dr. Thomas P. Stossel at Harvard Medical School and Brigham and Women's Hospital, the company develops recombinant human plasma gelsolin (rhu-pGSN) — a protein replacement therapy built on plasma gelsolin (pGSN), an abundant naturally occurring human blood protein that acts as a master regulator of the innate immune response by keeping inflammation localized to the site of injury and boosting the body's ability to clear pathogens. Its lead programme, BTI-203, is a 600-patient, 75-site, 13-country randomized double-blind placebo-controlled Phase 2 study of rhu-pGSN in moderate-to-severe acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), supported by a $20M BARDA DRIVe contract (75A50123C00067); a second Phase 2 programme targets inflammasome-driven decompression sickness under a U.S. Navy Office of Naval Research contract with the University of Maryland School of Medicine. The FDA has granted Fast Track designation for both ARDS and DCS indications. BioAegis holds an exclusive worldwide license from Brigham and Women's Hospital and more than 40 issued patents spanning inflammatory disease, infection, renal failure, neurologic disease and frailty. BioAegis Therapeutics runs no developer program and publishes no product API, developer portal or API documentation. The only machine-readable surface reachable without credentials is the WordPress REST content API behind www.bioaegistherapeutics.com, catalogued here.
