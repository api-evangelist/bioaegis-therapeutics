## What this company is

- Founded 2011; privately held; clinical stage. Headquarters: NJ Bioscience Center, 685 US Route 1, North Brunswick, NJ 08902.
- Platform: plasma gelsolin (pGSN), an abundant naturally occurring human blood protein that keeps inflammation localized to the site of injury and boosts the innate immune system's ability to clear pathogens. The therapeutic is the recombinant human form, rhu-pGSN, supplementing depleted levels rather than suppressing immunity.
- Founding science: the late Dr. Thomas P. Stossel (Harvard Medical School / Brigham and Women's Hospital), who discovered gelsolin. BioAegis holds an exclusive worldwide license from Brigham and Women's Hospital and more than 40 issued patents.
- Lead programme: BTI-203, a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 2 study of rhu-pGSN added to standard of care in moderate-to-severe ARDS (P/F ratio <= 150) caused by infection. 600 patients, 75 sites, 13 countries (US, Canada, UK and EU). Primary endpoint: survival without organ failure at Day 28. Supported by BARDA DRIVe contract 75A50123C00067 ($20M).
- Second programme: Phase 2 in inflammasome-driven decompression sickness (DCS), under a U.S. Navy Office of Naval Research contract awarded to the University of Maryland School of Medicine.
- FDA Fast Track designation granted for both ARDS and DCS.
- Announced April 2026: a collaboration with Prenosis to advance AI-driven precision medicine in inflammatory disease.

- Change detection: `GET /wp-json/wp/v2/posts?_fields=id,modified&orderby=modified&order=desc&per_page=1`

### Traps

- ALWAYS send `_fields`. `content.rendered` is populated and page 5753 (Our Platform) alone returns ~100KB of rendered Elementor markup; an unfiltered `per_page=100` is a multi-megabyte response.
- `per_page` is capped at 100. Values outside 1-100 return 400 `rest_invalid_param`.
- Totals live only in headers — `X-WP-Total`, `X-WP-TotalPages` and RFC 8288 `Link`. The body is a bare array with no envelope.
- `author` is a dead foreign key. /wp/v2/users returns 403 from the Sucuri WAF as an HTML page, not JSON, so authorship cannot be resolved and `_embed` on the author relation returns an error stub. Branch on Content-Type before parsing any error body.
- Errors are the WordPress `{code, message, data.status}` envelope, not RFC 9457 problem+json. WordPress returns 401 (not 403) for capability refusals to anonymous callers, so retry-with-credentials logic will loop.
- No ETag, Last-Modified or Cache-Control. Conditional requests are impossible; use each object's `modified` field.
- The `yoast/v1/get_head` operation exists only because the Yoast SEO plugin is installed. It would vanish without notice if the plugin were changed.
- The WordPress Abilities API (`wp-abilities/v1`), an agent-facing capability registry, IS registered on this deployment — but every endpoint under it returns 401 anonymously. There is no usable agent surface here.
